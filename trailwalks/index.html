About TrailWalks

Find local walks across the UK, record and plan your own trails, and count your steps, all in one place.

TrailWalks is made by one person who loves to explore. If you think anything needs adding or changing, please leave a review.

Features

Find walks

  • Walks near you and right across the UK
  • Hill walks and coastal paths, such as the Fife Coastal Path
  • Search for places and trails

Hill walks

  • Thousands of UK peaks and hill paths built in
  • Works even with a poor signal

Record & plan

  • GPS recording of your walks
  • Create your own routes and keep them in My Trails
  • Compass for finding your way

Steps & achievements

  • Daily step count
  • Badges and streaks
  • Keep your hiking achievements in one place
