About ShiftPay

ShiftPay is a rota planner and pay estimator for UK shift workers, built for 12-hour patterns. You can plan your rota, see what each shift earns, estimate your take-home pay and keep track of your holidays, all on your phone.

Features

Rota calendar

  • Preset 12-hour patterns, or build your own
  • Colour-coded days with bank holidays marked
  • Log swaps, overtime, holiday and sickness on any day

Pay per shift

  • Rates for nights, weekends and bank holidays
  • Overtime worked out for you
  • See what each shift earns

Take-home estimate

  • UK 2026/27 tax and NI, including Scottish tax
  • Pension and student loan deductions
  • An estimate only, so always check your payslip

Holidays & reminders

  • Track your allowance, booked days and what’s left
  • Reminders before shifts and sleep reminders on nights
  • Share your rota with a link

Private

  • No account, no ads, no tracking
  • Everything stays on your phone
