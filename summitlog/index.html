Summit Log Support

Summit Log is a hill bagging app covering all 20,000+ hills in the UK, the Isle of Man and the Channel Islands. That includes 30 lists, such as Munros, Corbetts, Grahams, Donalds, Wainwrights, Birketts, Hewitts, Nuttalls, Deweys, Marilyns, HuMPs, Simms, Tumps and county tops. All the hill data is built in, so it works offline.

Contact

If you have a question, find a bug or want to suggest a feature, email trailwalksapp@gmail.com.

Common questions

Is my log backed up? Only when you back it up yourself. Use Settings, then Export backup, and keep the file somewhere safe. Photos aren't included.

Where does the hill data come from? The Database of British and Irish Hills (hill-bagging.co.uk/dobih), CC BY 4.0.
